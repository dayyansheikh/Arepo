Assumptions and limitations
Assumptions
  • A signal fires when composite anomaly strength is 0.6 or higher.
  • A "hit" means price moved 0.03 probability points or more in the signalled direction within 5 frames.
  • Signal generation uses frames 0 through i; evaluation uses frames i+1 through i+H only.
  • No transaction costs, slippage or fees are modelled.
Limitations
  • Deterministic synthetic replay dataset — results do not generalise to live markets.
  • Small sample; no survivorship correction for markets that have closed.
  • Hit rate measures follow-through only, not profitability or alpha.
Astrolabe is a read-only research tool over public market data. It does not place trades, offer financial advice or prove insider activity. Signals flag statistically unusual behaviour for further reading, not evidence of wrongdoing or a claim of profitability.
