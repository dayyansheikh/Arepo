What is the composite anomaly signal?

It blends three things that are each individually normal to watch in any market: how far the price has moved from its recent average, how wide the bid-ask spread has become, and how imbalanced the order book looks. When several of these line up at once, the composite score rises and a signal fires.

How thresholds affect the signal

Raising the strength threshold means fewer, higher-confidence signals; lowering it surfaces more candidates but with a higher false positive rate. A wider z-score window smooths out short-lived noise but reacts more slowly to genuine regime change. Test the effect of any combination in Replay.
