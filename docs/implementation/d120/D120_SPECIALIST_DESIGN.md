# D120 specialist design: reliable, independent, category-agnostic trigger/control pairing (Opus 5.5 max, 2026-10-11)

Read-only design analysis. It is not frozen and is pending a Fable 5.1 review. All figures are design estimates from a
seeded model; the analysis scripts are in `scripts/` and must be regenerated by a committed, pinned script before
any protocol cites a number.

## Root cause (D118 had 2 of 7 triggers with a control)
- The cause is structural. Each stratum screens 3 members, controls are matched one-to-one in the same stratum, and the
  frozen trigger fires on about 56% of quoted books.
  - Under the best-supported model (S4), the expected fill was 0.37 and the observed fill 0.29.
  - Dead books and timing played no part in D118.
- **Counterfactual fill:**

  | Change | Fill |
  |---|---|
  | D118 as designed | 0.37 |
  | Shared controls allowed | 0.57 |
  | One-to-one, k = 12 | 0.55 |
  | One-to-one, k = 100 | 0.66 (ceiling about (1−p)/p) |
- **The trigger state is persistent:** 94.7% unchanged after about 80 s, and P(untriggered | triggered) = 4.7%.
- **Independence trap:** `event_group` is None (`selection.py:180`), and it sits inside the stratum key (`sampling.py:198-202`).
  Filling it in naively would force sibling pairs, so it must become a dependence attribute instead.
- **Data already in the frame rows:**
  - `events[0].id` is present on 100% of rows;
  - `feeType` is present on 99.5%;
  - the series slug is present on 84%;
  - `negRiskMarketID` never spans two events.
- **D118 pairs:** pair 1 is confirmed siblings (event 681898). Pair 2 spans two events and is independent.

## Category
Category comes from the frame `feeType`, mapped by prefix through a frozen, versioned table:
- sports, politics, crypto, weather, finance, economics, culture, tech and mentions;
- `other:<raw>`;
- `unknown` (0.5%).

Category is never inferred from question text. Tags are not available in keyset rows.

## Recommended design (for D120)
- **Population:** temporal ∩ liquidity ≥ 1000, kept as a declared parameter.
- **Category policy:** all categories by default, with an optional exclude list. Unknown is its own stratum.
- **Drawable strata:** those with at least 8 dependence groups (134 of 298; 97.1% of members kept).
- **Dependence group:** the union of event id, negRiskMarketID and series slug (3,759 groups).
- **Draw:**
  - draw 6 strata uniformly at random;
  - in each, screen 16 groups, one member per group, with a shared claim set across strata;
  - the trigger is the first available triggered member (seeded hash); the control is the first untriggered member;
  - activate pairs only (at most 12 origins);
  - record exact conditional inclusion probabilities.
- **Windows:** taken only for activated members, after a fresh `gamma.market` + `clob.book` binding read.
- **Unchanged:** trigger rule, freshness, windows, target and concurrency.
- **Timing estimates:** screening about 28 s live; oldest assessment at activation about 59 s against the 120 s limit; window age at freeze about 26 s live / 36 s synthetic; audit about 100 s against 300 s; about 13 min end to end.
- **Storage:** actual use is about 3.1 GB. The existing reservation ceilings would demand about 24 GB, so a reviewed, measured profile (about 9–10 GB) is needed.
- **Blockers:**
  1. The Phase 3 frame (`frame.py`) has no Cloudflare-cursor detour. A versioned frame-contract change is needed.
  2. A storage reservation profile.
  3. New modules: `pairing_*`, `d120_evaluator.py`, scripts, a new original-reader version. Existing D116/D118 files stay untouched.
- **D120 criteria (single run):**
  - **PASS:** fill ≥ 0.8 with ≥ 4 triggers in scope, and ≥ 3 independent complete pairs.
  - **Hard gates:** no shared dependence group, exact replay, and the D116-style gates.
  - **PASS WITH LIMITATION:** `control_scarcity`, which routes to the "screen the whole stratum" fallback.
  - **INCONCLUSIVE:** fewer than 4 strata with a trigger.
  - **Design P(PASS):** about 0.5–1.0.
- **What a PASS would license:** registered trigger-vs-control effect studies on this population with a declared category set.
  - Within-stratum paired estimands, clustered by run and series.
  - Excluding sports is a parameter change, which needs regenerated design figures but no new validation.
  - Not licensed: effect claims from D120 itself, or any other population.
- **Risks:**
  - The persistent trigger means the contrast is largely asymmetric vs symmetric (often market-maker) books, so Phase 4 must register covariates and balance checks.
  - Residual dependence through common underlyings.
  - The liquidity floor is still post hoc.
  - About 200 requests per run.
