# User-authorised bounded local Phase3 retention — October10

The user explicitly authorises compacting completed failed/superseded bulk local research
runs after a durable sufficient evidence capsule exists. This supersedes the prior blanket
requirement to retain every failed discovery frame forever. Production retention, accepted
canonical evidence, frozen cohorts/origins/targets and uncertain/unresolved material stay
protected. This permission does not authorise paid storage or production changes.

Before deletion retain purpose, original code/protocol/script hashes, population/exclusion
counts, actual clocks, selection seed/strata/probabilities/roles (or explicitly not reached),
all gate results, errors/timing, findings/design consequences, raw subset paths/hashes, a
complete deletion manifest and measured reclaimed space. Keep the smallest raw subset that
supports the finding and referenced regressions. Stop if a dependency is uncertain. Retain
at least one suitable full canonical/reference frame and all current active/unresolved data.

A capsule is not archive equivalence. Retiring bulk data may intentionally end whole-frame
original-code replay; record that limitation plainly. Old recorded verification results remain
historical results, not a claim that deleted inputs can still be reverified. Never silently
admit a partial retired journal as a complete fresh frame. No outcome or failed status changes.

Initial candidates: pre-selection D101/D106/D110 bulk journals; their timeout/timing diagnoses
are resolved, code/evidence preserved, and D114 supplies a current full frame with successful
original verification. Retain root manifests/reports, all known profile/benchmark pages,
failed attempts and boundary/large-payload examples. Preserve wrapper logs, timing sidecars,
read reports and failed selection declarations outside these journals. Canonical D082 and
history pilots D094/D097, plus unresolved D114, remain untouched in this first compaction.
