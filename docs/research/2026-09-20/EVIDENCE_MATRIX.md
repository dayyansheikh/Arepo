# AREPO scientific evidence matrix

A targeted scoping review, not an exhaustive systematic review. Read depth and uncertainty are explicit. HYP in the feature register means an untested AREPO mechanism. Literature is evidence about its own setting, not a positive AREPO result.

## E01 — Prediction Markets (2004)

[Primary source](https://doi.org/10.3386/w10504). **Inspection:** Scite abstract/metadata. **Grade:** Foundational; transfer to AREPO unproven.

**Setting and method:** Prediction markets and forecasting applications; Review/theory.

**Finding:** Markets can aggregate dispersed information under conditions discussed by the authors.

**Limits:** Outcome-forecasting evidence; not a test of short-horizon trading alpha.

**AREPO experiment:** Market probability as resolution baseline; H39.

## E02 — Prediction Markets in the Laboratory (2013)

[Primary source](https://doi.org/10.1111/joes.12015). **Inspection:** Scite abstract/metadata. **Grade:** Mixed/context dependent.

**Setting and method:** Laboratory market designs; Review of experiments.

**Finding:** Design, information and participant incentives affect information aggregation.

**Limits:** Laboratory settings do not establish live Polymarket net returns.

**AREPO experiment:** Test regime and incentive interactions; H42.

## E03 — Bid, ask and transaction prices in a specialist market with heterogeneously informed traders (1985)

[Primary source](https://doi.org/10.1016/0304-405X(85)90044-3). **Inspection:** Scite abstract; full text access restricted. **Grade:** Established theoretical mechanism.

**Setting and method:** Theoretical specialist market; Adverse-selection model.

**Finding:** Informed trading can generate a spread even for a competitive risk-neutral specialist.

**Limits:** Mechanism, not a Polymarket effect estimate; retrieved abstract only.

**AREPO experiment:** Spread and adverse-selection controls; H25.

## E04 — The Price Impact of Order Book Events (2013/2014)

[Primary source](https://doi.org/10.1093/jjfinec/nbt003). **Inspection:** Scite excerpts/abstract and primary preprint. **Grade:** Strong adjacent-market evidence.

**Setting and method:** 50 US stocks; Order-flow imbalance and depth; short-interval price impact.

**Finding:** Observed price changes relate to order-flow imbalance and depth.

**Limits:** Much of the relation is contemporaneous; not proof of future continuation or profits.

**AREPO experiment:** Pre-trade depth; separate immediate impact and later response; H01,H02,H10,H23.

## E05 — Queue Imbalance as a One-Tick-Ahead Price Predictor in a Limit Order Book (2016)

[Primary source](https://doi.org/10.1142/S2382626616500064). **Inspection:** Scite first 6000 characters of full text plus excerpts. **Grade:** Suggestive transferable mechanism.

**Setting and method:** 10 liquid Nasdaq stocks; next midpoint tick; Logistic predictor; out-of-sample evaluation.

**Finding:** Queue imbalance predicts next price direction; stronger for large-tick stocks.

**Limits:** Different venue, tick regime and horizon; no general six-hour implication.

**AREPO experiment:** H08,H09,H27,H43.

## E06 — Cross-Impact of Order Flow Imbalance in Equity Markets (2021)

[Primary source](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2112.13213). **Inspection:** Scite abstract/excerpts. **Grade:** Adjacent empirical evidence.

**Setting and method:** Equity order books; Integrated and cross-asset OFI.

**Finding:** Studies incremental cross-impact and lagged relationships.

**Limits:** No verified Polymarket magnitude; asynchronous clocks and common news matter.

**AREPO experiment:** H15,H23.

## E07 — Optimal Execution with Dynamic Order Flow Imbalance (2015)

[Primary source](https://doi.org/10.1137/140992254). **Inspection:** Scite abstract/excerpts. **Grade:** Methodological.

**Setting and method:** Execution model; Stochastic control with flow imbalance.

**Finding:** Flow state can affect execution choices and adverse-selection costs.

**Limits:** Execution optimisation is different from demonstrating forecast alpha.

**AREPO experiment:** Execution and fill modelling; no standalone alpha claim.

## E08 — Unravelling the Probabilistic Forest: Arbitrage in Prediction Markets (2025)

[Primary source](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2508.03474). **Inspection:** Primary HTML methods/results sections. **Grade:** Preprint; suggestive.

**Setting and method:** Historical Polymarket linked contracts; Market-rebalancing and combinatorial arbitrage analysis.

**Finding:** Authors estimate substantial historical arbitrage extraction (about $40m).

**Limits:** Retrospective reconstruction, execution assumptions and protocol era limit replication; not an AREPO profit estimate.

**AREPO experiment:** H15,H36,H37,H38; test depth and actual conversion paths.

## E09 — Arbitrage Analysis in Polymarket NBA Markets (2026)

[Primary source](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2605.00864). **Inspection:** Primary HTML methods/results. **Grade:** Preprint; narrow evidence.

**Setting and method:** 173 NBA games, 75,088,497 snapshots, 4 Feb–4 Mar 2026; Synchronous book scanning across related sports markets.

**Finding:** Reports seven in-game binary opportunities with median duration about 3.6 seconds; excludes many post-game raw episodes.

**Limits:** Polling intervals similar to duration; snapshots do not prove fills; limited sport and month.

**AREPO experiment:** H36,H37,H58; reject post-event false opportunity.

## E10 — Decomposing Crowd Wisdom: Domain-Specific Calibration Dynamics in Prediction Markets (2026)

[Primary source](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2602.19520). **Inspection:** Primary HTML methods/results. **Grade:** Preprint; heterogeneous/partly null.

**Setting and method:** Kalshi and Polymarket; reported 292m trades and 327k contracts; Domain/horizon calibration and transaction-size analysis.

**Finding:** Calibration patterns vary; a trade-size effect did not replicate clearly on Polymarket.

**Limits:** Final-resolution calibration differs from repricing; dependence and domain composition matter.

**AREPO experiment:** H06,H07,H34,H39; reject universal whale/category rules.

## E11 — Anatomy of a Blockchain Prediction Market: The 2024 US Presidential Election (2026)

[Primary source](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2603.03136). **Inspection:** Primary abstract. **Grade:** Preprint; narrow.

**Setting and method:** 2024 election market; Market evolution and blockchain trading analysis.

**Finding:** Studies how liquidity and price impact change as the market matures.

**Limits:** One highly salient event; no universal market-age effect established.

**AREPO experiment:** H41; event-level validation.

## E12 — The Ghosts of Polymarket: When Off-Chain Matches Meet On-Chain Reverts (2026)

[Primary source](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2606.16852). **Inspection:** Primary abstract and Scite excerpts. **Grade:** Preprint; measurement warning.

**Setting and method:** Hybrid off-chain matching/on-chain settlement; Record reconciliation and settlement failure analysis.

**Finding:** Off-chain matches and final settlement need not be equivalent observations.

**Limits:** Current mitigation status and prevalence not independently established here; no exploit replication.

**AREPO experiment:** H57; source reconciliation and settlement states.

## E13 — Polymarket-v1 Database (2026)

[Primary source](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2606.04217). **Inspection:** Primary abstract and Scite excerpts. **Grade:** Dataset preprint.

**Setting and method:** Reported 1.20bn records; 21 Nov 2022–28 Apr 2026; On-chain v1 exchange reconstruction.

**Finding:** Provides a long historical record with transaction-level measurement distinctions.

**Limits:** Ends before current v2 era; on-chain history lacks AREPO receipt clocks and full public book history.

**AREPO experiment:** Historical mechanism development only; protocol-era holdout.

## E14 — Fill-Side Behavioral Concentration on Polymarket: Identification Limits under Record-Level Attribution (2026)

[Primary source](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2605.11640). **Inspection:** Scite abstract/excerpts. **Grade:** Preprint; identification caution.

**Setting and method:** Polymarket fill records; Attribution and behavioural concentration analysis.

**Finding:** Fill-side address records have limits for identifying trader roles and concentration.

**Limits:** Address is not person; quote lifecycle and private hedges are not observed.

**AREPO experiment:** H05,H11,H33; retain attribution uncertainty.

## E15 — Empirical Evaluation of Deadline-Resolved Information Leakage on Documented Polymarket Insider Cases (2026)

[Primary source](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2605.02286). **Inspection:** Scite abstract/excerpts. **Grade:** Preprint; selected cases.

**Setting and method:** Selected documented cases; Event-time alignment around public information and resolution.

**Finding:** Apparent information advantages can depend on whether underlying news was already public.

**Limits:** Case selection cannot estimate population skill; public-event timestamps require scrutiny.

**AREPO experiment:** H03,H13,H40; distinguish pre-event from settlement window.

## E16 — Per-Market Information Leakage and Order-Flow Skill: Two Methodological Lenses on Informed Trading in Decentralized Prediction Markets (2026)

[Primary source](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2605.02287). **Inspection:** Primary abstract, revised v2. **Grade:** Preprint; conceptual distinction.

**Setting and method:** Comparison of account and market-level screens; Methodological comparison.

**Finding:** Account skill, unusual lifecycle patterns and event-level information timing answer different questions.

**Limits:** Not a new prospective validation of a trading signal; insider classification precision can be unknown.

**AREPO experiment:** Wallet skill test and public anomaly labelling, not accusations.

## E17 — OpenMarket: A Synchronized Polymarket-Binance Dataset for High-Frequency Prediction-Market Research (2026)

[Primary source](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2607.26245). **Inspection:** Primary abstract and repository README. **Grade:** Preprint/dataset; valuable negative result.

**Setting and method:** Reported 727m rows; model panel 2,251 markets; paired clock observations; Timestamp-aware synchronisation and forecast benchmark.

**Finding:** Repository reports strong headline AUC but no improvement over market midpoint prior and negative simulated results after costs.

**Limits:** Author-reported benchmark, limited crypto setting; independently rerunning was outside this audit.

**AREPO experiment:** Clock architecture; strong baselines; H14,H44.

## E18 — Prediction Markets, Social Media and Information Efficiency (2016)

[Primary source](https://doi.org/10.1111/kykl.12119). **Inspection:** Scite abstract; full text restricted. **Grade:** Suggestive case evidence.

**Setting and method:** Twitter/Betfair around UK 2010 Bigotgate; Information diffusion case analysis.

**Finding:** Reports a role for corroboration and delayed response in a particular event.

**Limits:** One dated event and platform; retrieved fulltext request returned abstract only.

**AREPO experiment:** H18,H20,H52; prospective timestamping.

## E19 — Giving Content to Investor Sentiment: The Role of Media in the Stock Market (2007)

**Setting and method:** Equity market and newspaper text; Sentiment/time-series analysis.

**Finding:** Media pessimism relates to price pressure and subsequent reversal in the study setting.

## E20 — All the News That's Fit to Reprint: Do Investors React to Stale Information? (2011)

**Finding:** Stale/repeated information has different response and reversal characteristics.

**Limits:** Historical equities; text novelty definition and timing require prospective adaptation.

**AREPO experiment:** H17,H28,H52,H54.

## E21 — In Search of Attention (2011)

[Primary source](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1540-6261.2011.01679.x). **Inspection:** Scite abstract. **Grade:** Adjacent empirical evidence.

**Setting and method:** Google search and equity activity; Search-volume attention proxy.

**Finding:** Search attention can measure a different dimension from standard market activity.

**Limits:** Query scaling, access and population changed; not evidence of current PM edge.

**AREPO experiment:** H18,H49,H50.

## E22 — Using Stacking to Average Bayesian Predictive Distributions (2018)

[Primary source](https://doi.org/10.1214/17-BA1091). **Inspection:** Scite first 6000 full-text characters and excerpts. **Grade:** Established methodological framework.

**Setting and method:** Predictive distribution combination; Stacking via predictive score optimisation.

**Finding:** Combination can be framed as predictive performance in an imperfect model set.

**Limits:** Standard LOO methodology needs chronological/grouped adaptation; no guaranteed win.

**AREPO experiment:** E50/EW/ES/EG tournament; H60.

## E23 — On the Forecast Combination Puzzle (2019)

[Primary source](https://doi.org/10.3390/econometrics7030039). **Inspection:** Scite abstract/excerpts. **Grade:** Methodological.

**Setting and method:** Forecast-combination methodology; Simple versus estimated weighting.

**Finding:** Weight estimation uncertainty helps explain why simple averaging can be competitive.

**Limits:** Not a theorem that 50/50 is optimal for AREPO.

**AREPO experiment:** Always include fixed 50/50 and shrink fitted weights.

## E24 — Sparsity information and regularization in the horseshoe and other shrinkage priors (2017)

[Primary source](https://doi.org/10.1214/17-EJS1337SI). **Inspection:** Scite first 6000 full-text characters. **Grade:** Established method.

**Setting and method:** Sparse Bayesian regression; Regularised horseshoe and prior effective model size.

**Finding:** Regularisation can limit weakly identified large coefficients and encode sparsity expectations.

**Limits:** Prior choice remains substantive; does not solve leakage or event dependence.

**AREPO experiment:** Hierarchical feature/wallet models and prior sensitivity.

## E25 — Bayesian Workflow (2020)

[Primary source](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2011.01808). **Inspection:** Primary/Scite abstract. **Grade:** Methodological preprint.

**Setting and method:** Bayesian model development; Workflow and diagnostics.

**Finding:** Prior/posterior checks, computation and model criticism belong in the workflow.

**Limits:** A workflow cannot establish a market edge without suitable data.

**AREPO experiment:** Prior predictive checks, diagnostics and revision log.

## E26 — Strictly Proper Scoring Rules, Prediction, and Estimation (2007)

[Primary source](https://doi.org/10.1198/016214506000001437). **Inspection:** Scite metadata/abstract. **Grade:** Established theory.

**Setting and method:** Probabilistic forecasting; Theory of proper scores.

**Finding:** Proper scoring aligns evaluation with honest predictive distributions.

**Limits:** Finite-sample dependence and practical economic utility still need separate treatment.

**AREPO experiment:** Brier/log loss; no accuracy-only model selection.

## E27 — On Calibration of Modern Neural Networks (2017)

[Primary source](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1706.04599). **Inspection:** Primary/Scite abstract. **Grade:** Established adjacent method.

**Setting and method:** Neural classification benchmarks; Calibration evaluation and temperature scaling.

**Finding:** Predictive accuracy and probability calibration are distinct.

**Limits:** Different models/data; calibration method must be tested with chronological data.

**AREPO experiment:** Fold-local calibration; no final-test recalibration.

## E28 — The probability of backtest overfitting (2016)

[Primary source](https://doi.org/10.21314/JCF.2016.322). **Inspection:** Scite abstract/excerpts. **Grade:** Methodological warning.

**Setting and method:** Strategy selection research; Backtest selection bias analysis.

**Finding:** Searching many specifications can create misleading apparent winners.

**Limits:** Specific proposed validation techniques do not license random time splits here.

**AREPO experiment:** Trial ledger, locked prospective confirmation.

## E29 — Hawkes processes in finance (2015)

[Primary source](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1502.04592). **Inspection:** Primary abstract. **Grade:** Adjacent methodology.

**Limits:** Complex intensity models may fit reporting artefacts; no PM benefit established.
