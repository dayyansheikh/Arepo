## MUST (before J0 runs)

1. **Side counts leak data from after k (void 12.2 risk).** The side counts at l.257-259 go into the status files that J1 writes and others read during the window (l.682). Both J0 and J1 compute them, so `crossed_book` and `wide_book_high_ask` include rows of units, and of J0's hypothetical units, after their k. Append to l.259: "Side counts at capture j are taken over markets **not in the near-seen set at j**, so no unit's (or hypothetical unit's) row after its k enters any count."
2. **The J0 status-forms count is unscoped.** At l.654 it could tally post-k `propos` forms of hypothetical units. Replace with: "the forms of the status string **on candidate rows at E6 only** (distinct normalised forms, every non-whitelist form uncapped)".
3. **The 252-slot figure is hard-coded.** Rule (a) may change only the window length (l.24-26). So the doubling has to be declared now, or applying (a) would change a threshold outside (a). At l.792 write "75% of the window's slots (252; 504 under 10.1 (a))", and at l.112 write "(21 days, 252 slots; 42 days, 504 slots under 10.1 (a))".
4. **Rule (b)'s claim "(a) always fires first" (l.666) is true only if the projection is median × 21.** Write: "(b) record only: the projected 21-day count = median full-day units × 21 < 1,008, which is identical to (a) by construction."
