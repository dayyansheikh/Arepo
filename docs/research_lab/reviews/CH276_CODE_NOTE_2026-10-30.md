## 2. What is read, what is never read

- Never opened: the engine's `logs/` (the resolution store), the monitor's `alerts` / `scores` / `outcomes` / `wallets` files, any `labels`, `holdout` path (`guard_path`); never requested: any column named `y`, `closed_time`, `res_7d`, or containing `payoff`, `pnl`, `profit`, `winner`, `wallet`, `proxy`, `label` (`guard_columns` checks the columns the code ASKS for in every csv / parquet / jsonl read; it does not inspect a file's full column set). A test spies on every `open` / `gzip.open` / `pq.read_table` and asserts none of this, and that decoy files with those names are not opened.
- The monitor's print records are reduced to `ts`, `condition_id`, `side`, `outcome_index`, `cash` (`recv_ts` is declared but unused). `cash` is compared with the $1,000 floor only; `price`, `size`, `wallet_hash` are never read.
- Post-k reads, each named in section E: (4) the k + 1 sidecar bid or ask of an eligible row that is one-sided at k + 1, compared with `mid_k +- 0.005` and discarded (a sign class, not a value); the k + 1 quote status of every eligible row; (5) print counts in (`t_k`, `t_exit`]; (7) whether a later capture shows a proposal flag; (8) the sidecar status string at each capture (disputed first appearance and run length); (5) also uses "absent from the open listing" for `t_exit`. Nothing else after k.
- No fitted value, no p-value, no edge / profit language: `check_output` refuses an output key matching `p_value`, `edge`, `alpha`, `sharpe`, `profit`, `pnl`, `t_stat`, `z_score`. The only SD printed is the descriptive within-event and between-event SD of the champion's `s_2h` that CH270's census line asks for.
- A test (`test_no_post_k_value_changes_the_counts`) rebuilds the store with the prices of the unit markets after their k, the engine mids from k = 32 and every monitor print's price and size changed, and asserts lines 2, 4 (states), 5, 6, 7, 8 are identical.

## 3. Ambiguities and the reading chosen (conservative where there was a choice)

1. **"the number of members with any status" / "unflagged sibling B's row carries A's proposal" (line 1).** Read as: at the capture where a market A first shows a proposal (code 1, previous capture empty), the other open rows of A's `neg_risk_market_id` group; a sibling "carries the proposal" if its own status string contains `propos`. `semantics_reading` = `per_market` below a sibling-carry share of 0.10, `event_level` above 0.90, else `mixed`. The 0.10 / 0.90 edges are NOT in the text; they are a reading aid (M1-J0's 85 flagged losers against about 5,000 siblings is about 2%) and are printed with the share, so a reviewer can re-cut it. The list-length-against-group-size and against-members-with-status counts are printed raw beside it.
2. **"neg-risk group size".** Open binary rows sharing a non-empty `neg_risk_market_id` at the capture (the exclusive group, not `event_id`; CH270 joins by `event_id` and says the group key is for events that mix groups, so line 3 uses `event_id`, line 1 and 6 use the group id).
3. **Line 2 "proposal rows" and "favoured side".** Proposal rows = first-seen proposals in scope at the M1 funnel's scope test (so the rows are `not_two_sided` + `contested` + `unit`; only `not_two_sided` is split). A one-sided book has no mid, so the favoured side is read from the surviving price (>= 0.90 = YES, <= 0.10 = NO, between = `mid_zone`); a bid-only row at 0.02 therefore reads as NO-favoured. Crossed / locked and no-quote books are counted apart. Tier is the sidecar `liquidity` tier of M1-J0.
4. **Line 3 "eligible".** The engine's eligibility table is not read. Eligible = features `category` not in sports / crypto / unknown (CH269's convention), `dir_2h` eligibility restated from `end_ts` (`labels.elig_mask`: `end_ts` missing or after the slot + 2 h target), and two-sided in `quotes/` at k (CH271 says the eligible unit is two-sided at k). `co_n >= 3` counts these rows by `event_id` among `ms_neg_risk > 0.5` rows. The within-event SD uses members with a finite champion `s_2h` (needs >= 3), sample SD (ddof 1); "median event" = the median over all event-capture SDs; the between-event SD is the SD of event means within a capture, median over captures. Counted over `--engine-days` (default 8), not the 35 prediction days.
5. **Line 4 states.** The text lists five; a sixth, `inactive` (quotes `st = 2`), is printed because it is a distinct engine code. Sign-determinable uses the text's `>=` / `<=` with a 1e-9 float slack (the engine's own label uses strict thresholds with a 1e-6 tolerance; a market exactly at the boundary is therefore counted here and would not be a label). A one-sided k + 1 row with no readable sidecar price (no capture of k + 1, or both sides missing) is `one_sided_indeterminate` and the missing-capture case is counted separately. `mid_k` is the quotes mid at k. By "mid bin" = CH240's four bins; hours-to-end bins = CH274's (< 3 d, 3-14 d, 14-60 d, >= 60 d, missing) from features `ms_hours_to_end` at k. The `--engine-days` window uses the quotes k + 1 of the last counted capture if it exists (it is read for status only).
6. **`ox_1s_84` window.** Text: "slots k - 84 .. k" with the unit two-sided at k. Read as the 84 slots k - 84 .. k - 1 (k itself is two-sided by eligibility). Missing quote slots are not one-sided; `history_slots_present_at_k` and a full-history-only share are printed so the decision is visible both ways. The ">= 2%" line is applied to all eligible rows. Illiquidity correlation (`|corr(ox_1s_84, ms_log_liq)| > 0.8`) is NOT computed (it is a correlation of a count with a feature, outside "counts only" as written in section E (4)); flagged in `not_mapped`-style reading below.
7. **Line 5 `t_k`, `t_exit`, fill flow.** `t_k` = the later of the row and sidecar receipts of M1's unit (M1's decision time without the sweep; the sweep is not opened). `t_exit` = start of the first later capture where the market is not in the open binary listing (absent, closed or inactive); units with no exit inside the span are `censored` and their window is cut at the last capture start; the decision count uses units whose window is inside the monitor's cycle lines (covered) and any print in the window (mapping-independent). The fill-flow mapping (favoured YES: SELL outcome 0 or BUY outcome 1; favoured NO: SELL outcome 1 or BUY outcome 0) is printed beside it, with every `side|outcome_index` form seen, because the text says the mapping is "pinned before the census from the monitor's own docstring" and that docstring does not state it. Windows are cut on venue time `ts`. The cycle-gap threshold (900 s, three nominal 5-minute cycles) is not in the text; gaps, their total seconds and the share of covered unit windows overlapping a gap are printed.
8. **Line 6 "B".** A = an M1 unit with favoured side YES and non-empty `neg_risk_market_id` (A with an empty group id is counted as `winner_units_with_empty_neg_risk_market_id`). B = every other open binary row of A's group, in scope, with no own status at k and no proposal at k - 1; one unit per B market. B rows with an own status are NOT B units (they are M1's `contested` / flagged funnel, counted as `own_status_at_k`). "Group size" = open binary rows of the group (bucketed 2, 3-5, 6-10, 11+). `no_bid` = ask-only. Moved / unmoved = the B bid rounded to 6 dp at k against k - 1 (only for B two-sided at both). `E_B` is a sidecar-side restatement (YES mid in [0.02, 0.98], two-sided, no own proposal) because the engine's `end_ts` eligibility is not read here. The augmented ("Other") flag is not a column read here: not mapped. Daily counts are by the UTC day of A's capture; the median is over full days.
9. **Line 7 population.** Implemented from CH274's text: non-sports ex-crypto, two-sided at k and k - 1, favoured ask >= 0.95 at k (YES ask, or NO ask = 1 - YES bid) and < 0.95 at k - 1, one unit per market, first crossing only, no status at k, `end_date` > k + 24 h, market age >= 12 slots. Age is from the sidecar `created_at` (the text does not name the field); a missing `created_at` makes the market `market_younger_than_12_slots_or_age_unknown` (excluded and counted). `born_certain` = first seen two-sided already >= 0.95 with age < 12 slots; `pre_window_certain_by_window_start` = already certain at the first fed capture or crossed during the 12 warm-up captures; `first_sight_certain_old_market` = first seen certain later in the span but older than 12 slots (a third class the text does not name, kept apart rather than added to either). [Superseded in revision 2: the rules class is the engine's point-in-time aux/rules table at k, and the proposed-before-`end_date` share is dropped; see section 5.]
10. **Line 8 insurance class.** `ins_k = 1 - bid_k` of the favoured side (YES: `1 - best_bid`; NO: `best_ask`), rounded to 6 dp; `uninsured` <= 0.002, `insured` > 0.010, `middle` between (the text's "reported arm"). Units are M1's (two-sided, favoured side >= 0.90, E1-E7), so the one-sided three quarters are in line 2, not here. Zone counts of `ins_k` use M1-J0's zones. "Scaled to 21 days" = insured count x 21 / span days. Dispute first appearance = a market's first contiguous run of captures whose status string contains `disput` (markets already disputed at the first fed capture are excluded and counted; runs that start in the warm-up are excluded and counted; a re-appearance after a run ended is counted apart, not a new first appearance). Duration = captures in that run (right-censored runs counted). "Disputes >= 1% of proposal events" has two denominators printed: events of first-seen in-scope proposal rows (the one used by `decisions.ch275`) and events of M1 units. The `lingering` and absent-at-k + 1 shares are NOT computed: they would read the post-k listing for a unit, which (8) does not name; flagged in the output.
11. **Line 9.** Spread = ask - bid of two-sided `quotes/` rows at 1e-4 resolution (median read from an exact histogram), tier from `expm1(ms_log_liq)`; printed for all categories and for the non-sports ex-crypto subset; periods default to the text's 10-13..10-19 and 10-21..10-29 UTC days, overridable. A capture missing in a period is not filled; `captures_read` against `expected_2h_slots` is printed.
12. **Decision lines.** `built` / `drafted` are the text's thresholds restated in `LINES` and printed (`decision_lines`); `ch272` uses the median per full day of units with any print in the window; `ch273` needs both >= 25 two-sided B units a day and `semantics_reading == "per_market"` (`mixed` or `event_level` gives false); a null count gives null. Nothing was tuned; the numbers are reported, not read against a result.
13. **Not computed though a census line in the entry text mentions it** (so the reviewer is not surprised): `|corr(ox_1s_84, ms_log_liq)|` (CH271), `|corr(co_dev_s2h, s_2h)|` (CH270), the rule-24 within / between stratum SD and the MDE / F-MDE (CH275). They are fitted or correlation quantities, not counts, and are not in section E's list.
14. **Peak memory** is unmeasured on host data. The synthetic RSS in the tests means nothing. `mem_mb` 450 is the instruction; CH269 ran 558.8 MB against 450. The host dry run must record `peak_rss_mb` before promotion.

### Semantics reading rule (to be declared in the ledger)

Unit: a GROUP at a capture where at least one open member first shows a proposal (status code 1, empty at the previous capture); A = the lowest market_id among the group's first-seen proposals at that capture, the group being the open binary rows sharing a non-empty neg_risk_market_id and having at least one other open member (a sibling). Counts are group-level and sibling-level at that capture only (nothing after it). Per sibling: 'identical' = its own status string is non-empty and equal to A's; 'unstatused' = its status is empty. event_level if (siblings identical / siblings) >= 0.99 (about 100%) AND the share of groups with at least one unstatused open sibling is below 0.5; per_market if the share of groups with at least one unstatused open sibling is >= 0.5 AND the identical-sibling share is below 0.99; every other case (both or neither condition) is mixed; no group or no sibling at all is null. mixed -> the CH273 decision is null, never False; event_level -> CH273 is False (its B population would carry the proposal flag); any null count -> the decision is null. The in-scope (non-sports ex-crypto, by A's category) reading is the one the decisions use; the all-category reading is printed beside it (sports dominates the venue). All thresholds are named constants printed in decision_lines; the raw counts are printed.

Named constants (printed in `decision_lines`): `SEM_EVENT_LEVEL_IDENTICAL_MIN` = 0.99 (the text's "about 100%"; the rule needs a number, to be confirmed when declared) and `SEM_PER_MARKET_GROUP_SHARE` = 0.5. Neither is tuned on data.
