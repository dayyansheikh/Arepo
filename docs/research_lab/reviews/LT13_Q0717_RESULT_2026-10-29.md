## Decision
- 1.4.3's hypotheses (ii) (the spans) and (v) (markets switching class between k - 1 and k) explain the shift: CH251's span
  straddles a one-time, venue-side change of the `fee_schedule` and `fee_type` values; CH261's span is almost all after it.
- Rule (b) asks for "a field-semantics cause that a declared reading rule fixes". This is not one: the vendored reading reads
  both regimes consistently and needs no fix; a rule that restored the pre-step classes would either make the documented rule
  primary (1.4.3 forbids it as a substitute) or read `fees_enabled = true` with a positive `taker_base_fee` as fee-on despite
  `rate 0` and `zero_fees`, a meaning the counts cannot establish and one chosen after seeing them.
- **Applied as written: CH252 `withdrawn_pre_census (fee reading unresolved)`** (uncounted, final, never `census_dropped`).
  **Rule (c): CH253 still goes to census** under R1 item 2 (stage A census code for CH253 alone). Rule (a): no reading rule added,
  no threshold changed.
- **Ambiguity flagged, not resolved silently:** the shift is now explained, so the declared label's word "unresolved" fits
  awkwardly; the rule's conjunction (explained AND a semantics cause AND a reading rule that fixes it) was read literally. A
  reviewer who reads (b) as "withdraw only if unexplained" would send CH252 to census instead; on q0717's levels its fee-on
  unrewarded non-sports ex-crypto population after the step is about 89-96 complete units a capture before any widening, tight or
  support filter (context only; not used in the decision).
