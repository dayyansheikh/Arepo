## Fixes (most severe first)
1. **§1.2, §8.3: the window's in-force rule must be decidable in real time.** At origin t, "no new-pid line yet" may mean r
   has not loaded (`L_obs` > t) or r adds no pid (`L_obs` unidentifiable); the reader cannot tell, and the rule is written
   as an after-the-fact correction while frozen lines are write-once. Fix: in the window r is in force at t only if the
   successor's `runs.jsonl` shows r loaded at a cycle <= t, **and** t >= `refreshed_utc` + L, **and** the reader had seen
   the file; the reader must not process origin t until the successor's `runs.jsonl` line for cycle t exists. `L_obs` is
   census-only; restate the "same rule" claim; add a unit test where the load comes late.
2. **§1.2, §10.1: L is declared, not guaranteed.** The Limiter retries with sleeps up to 60 s and 30 s timeouts
   (`history_backfill.py:231-260`); the Gamma page count can grow to `max_pages` 6000 = 12,000 s at 0.5 req/s
   (`cross_venue_live.py:481`). Say so; the census prints a lower bound on each refresh's duration,
   (`k_requests` + `p_requests`) / 0.5 s from `refresh.log`, and flags records where it exceeds L (counts only).
3. **§8.2 route 3, §1.4 item 2, §8.4 step 4: decisions still made after the verdict.** Triggers (a), (b), (d) are checked
   only after both analysis commits, each at owner / orchestrator discretion. Fix: take the `du` / free-disk reading and any
   owner approval of the host change, and merge the engine's second read root, before `T_E009`; afterwards (a) applies only
   on a new disk fact, (b) goes, (d) applies only on a recorded technical failure. Move the deadline for an owner scope
   decision from the seal to the design-text freeze (the seal comes after E009's verdict).
4. **§1.8.1, W10.** With 7 simulated days the day-cluster t has 6 df, dominates p = max(…) and can hide event-t oversize the
   21-day window would show: run W10 over 21 UTC days (source: the window length). The worlds drop 15% of origins i.i.d.,
   but real gaps are contiguous (a new pair loses about 3 h of its first day, retired pairs drop out, spread censoring
   clusters at jumps): add to W10 a share of pairs present only on contiguous sub-intervals, with neutral sources L and
   `RETIRE_KEEP_S`. With these, the `G_eff` >= 15 gate plus W10 handles the large-event case adequately.
5. **§9: broad-model gap in the embargo.** If `kx_*_2h` stay in the xvenue2 family and only isolate `kxbook`,
   `no_broad_families: ["kxbook"]` does not stop a broad model holding them. Move them into a `kxbook` family, or add
   xvenue2 to `no_broad_families` while it holds them. Extend the LT8/LT9 broad-spec test to cover LT10 (`CANDIDATES.md:207`).
6. **§13 preamble: disclose one easing.** The CD10 block-gate share used to need pair-day strata with >= 16 units; it now
   needs event-day strata with >= 16 rows and pairs with >= 4 units, which is easier to meet. It follows from must-fix 4 and
   is acceptable, but say so.
