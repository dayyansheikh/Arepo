## What this means for an LT12 design
- **CH245 is larger than planned**: 639 complete non-sports ex-crypto pairs a capture (1,279 member units) against the
  round-17 sketch of 100-400 events / 200-800 units; retention and completeness are not binding. Pairs are
  dominated by uu (60%) and rr (37%); ru is 3%. Per-pair dependence is the issue, not size: 1,023 distinct events in
  9.25 days.
- **CH244 is very large** (343k mixed-ladder captures in 21 days, 976 complete ladders a capture) and was unprobed;
  11+ ladders are only 31% complete, so a design must fix the completeness rule per member count.
- **CH243 sits on a gate**: 346 fee-free traded movers a capture on the mean but typical-capture medians of only 64 + 7, below the
  round-17 sketch of 100-250, with 213 more fee-free movers unclassed by missing or falling `volume_num` (against 990 classed). The 160 gate must be stated on a median or on a fresh seal, not this mean. The 21-day
  projection (87k) is above the sketch's 25-63k.
- **Missing fields a design must handle**: sidecar `volume_num` (traded / quote-only / unusable as a counted third class,
  fee-free more than fee-on); `fee_src` unknown; no tier definition (blocks the spec's matched control, CH250 tier cut);
  no label store (blocks CH242); no rules text (blocks CH246).
- The 10-13..10-23 span is disjoint from the LT11 seal; any LT12 gate is still to be declared before its own census.
