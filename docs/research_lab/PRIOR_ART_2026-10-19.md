# Prior art: documented prediction- and betting-market inefficiencies, mapped onto AREPO

Status: **literature map, research input only.** It contains no AREPO test, no claim of edge, calibration or
profitability, and it changes no protocol, threshold or gate. Every hypothesis in §4 is a candidate for the usual
creative-batch route: pre-registration, MDE, event clustering, Holm, challenger review and an economic check.
Compiled 2026-10-19 (UTC+1). Web sources were read on that date.

Evidence labels:
- **[PR]** peer-reviewed journal or proceedings.
- **[WP]** working paper or preprint (SSRN, NBER, CEPR, arXiv, theses). Many 2026 arXiv items are single-author and
  unrefereed.
- **[DOC]** venue documentation.
- **[PRAC]** practitioner, vendor, NGO or press analysis.
- **[ANEC]** single case.

Verification marks:
- **(v)** the numbers were checked on the primary source (full text or abstract).
- **(s)** the numbers come from secondary coverage or a search summary; check the source before citing them in a
  protocol.

AREPO status values:
- **TESTED**: a result exists, with its ID.
- **QUEUED**: designed in a creative batch (CB) or running, not yet scored.
- **IDEA**: a CHnn idea not in any active design.
- **GAP**: not represented.

**Cost yardstick.** The taker hurdle is about **3.5c** of spread plus fee against 0.3-1c of gross edge
(`CREATIVE_HYPOTHESES_2026-10-18.md`; SB2-J2).
- The median 5-share round trip is 4.5c (p25 3.2c, p75 6.8c); 1.8c at liquidity ≥100k; ≥6c below 1k
  (`EXECUTION_COSTS.md`, sweep of 2026-10-11).
- The fee is `C*rate*p(1-p)`, taker only, so it is near zero at the price extremes. The rate is 0.07 for crypto, 0.05
  for sports and most others, 0.04 for politics, finance, mentions and tech, and 0 for geopolitics.
- A hold-to-resolution position pays only the entry leg. A maker fill pays no fee and earns a rebate (about +0.1c per
  fill in ECON part B).

## 1. Executive summary

**Where AREPO stands.** AREPO has tested most of the classic catalogue. Results are null, not testable, or killed by
costs for:
- favourite-longshot bias (FLB) and calibration: C1, HB1 PX1-PX4, CB1-C
- tail-end carry: CB1-A
- deadline decay: IF-H2
- reversal: E002/E004
- flow continuation: E007, SB2-F4/F5
- neg-risk sums: E005, CB1-D
- generic wallet and unusual-flow signals: HB1 WA*, WALLET-FRESH, UNUSUAL_FLOW
- time of day: HB1 TI1-TI5
- 95 economic setups: ECON-95

AREPO's own replicated direction effects are 0.3-1c gross. The 2025-2026 literature is consistent with that wall:
nothing published shows a taker-side signal at AREPO-like horizons that survives costs.

**What the literature adds** is a set of effects that are larger than the wall, or that sit where the wall does not
apply (maker fills, hold to resolution, at price extremes):

1. **Informed trading is real and concentrated by class.** Military, geopolitical and privately-decided markets stand
   out.
2. **YES-optimism.** Takers buy YES, and single-name markets settle YES less often than priced, by about 4.5 pp on
   Kalshi. 78% of Polymarket markets resolve No.
3. **Political and long-horizon underconfidence.** Prices are compressed toward 50c and the compression grows with
   horizon. Polymarket and Kalshi both show it.
4. **Crypto strike and barrier tails sit 5-11 pp above option-implied probabilities.**
5. **Makers earn and takers lose** on Kalshi, Polymarket and Betfair, and venue programmes pay makers on top. AREPO
   has measured maker adverse selection, but never maker P&L with reward income included.

**Effects the literature now discounts:**
- **Within-market neg-risk arbitrage.** The $40M headline shrinks to about $1.1M executable (Gebele, Mutzel & Matthes
  2026).
- **Copy-trading P&L-ranked wallets.** It selects luck.
- **15-minute crypto latency arbitrage.** The evidence is anecdote, and fees since 2026-01 target it.
- **Cross-venue gaps.** About 2-6 pp, mostly from rule mismatch rather than information.

Honourable mentions, with reasons they rank lower:
- **UMA dispute and ambiguity premium.** Base rate ~0.40% of events (558 of 140,582 to Oct 2025) but rising (>1,150
  disputes in Jan-May 2026). LLMs cannot predict disputes ex ante (F1 ≤ 0.44). Nobody has tested whether ambiguity is
  priced. Cheap to describe; the evidence that an edge exists is still weak.
- **Weekend as a moderator.** Folded into gap 4 (H4c) and the E009/E010 follow-ups. The evidence alone is weak and old.
- **Within-market and combinatorial arbitrage.** Mostly not executable and seconds-lived. It is execution, not
  prediction.
- **Cross-venue Law of One Price gaps (2-4 pp).** Mostly rule mismatch. Live E009/E010 are already pending.

## 3. Reading the evidence honestly

1. **Venue and category specificity is the rule.**
   - FLB is strong on Kalshi in every category, but absent or reversed in Polymarket sports. It is absent on IEM,
     reversed in MLB/NHL moneylines, and weaker on Betfair than at bookmakers.
   - AREPO's C1 finding of no FLB at 1d (sample dominated by sports and crypto) agrees with Cardozo's sports result.
     It is not a contradiction of the literature.
2. **Weighting and clustering drive conclusions.**
   - Cardozo's Polymarket longshots are −19% pooled but **+4%** when each parent event gets equal weight.
   - Le finds about half the slope variation is noise once clustered by event.
   - This supports AREPO's event-clustered design. A headline FLB number should not be imported without event
     grouping.
3. **In cents per share, FLB is small.**
   - −19c per dollar on a ~5c contract is about 1c per share. +0.83c per dollar at ≥90c is about 0.8c per share.
   - These pay only on maker or hold-to-resolution routes in liquid markets, where the extreme-price round trip is
     under 1c.
   - The larger mid-price mispricings are YES-optimism (4.5 pp, Kalshi single-name) and long-horizon political
     compression (≈13 pp at 70c a week out, Le). That is why they rank above the classic FLB.
4. **Informed-flow evidence is large but selected.**
   - Mitts & Ofir's and ACDC's win rates come from sets flagged using timing and outcome information. Only a
     prospective, ex-ante trigger answers whether following them pays after latency.
   - Persistence of skill is contested: 44% (Gómez-Cram) against weak or modest (Akey).
   - All of these studies reuse overlapping 2024-2026 Polymarket on-chain histories, so they are not independent
     confirmations.
5. **Arbitrage headlines shrink under executability.** $39.6M becomes about $1.1M. This is consistent with AREPO's
   E005 and CB1-D nulls at 2h cadence.
6. **Cross-venue leadership conflicts.**
   - The literature (2024 election) has Polymarket and Betfair leading.
   - AREPO (non-election pairs, 10-min candles) finds Kalshi leading by 10-20 min.
   - Both can hold, by category and period. E009/E010 are the prospective arbiter.
7. **Nothing cited is a prospective, out-of-sample test with executable costs.** By AREPO's standard, all of it is
   development evidence.
8. **The venue keeps changing the rules.**
   - Fees started 2026-01-05 and widened through 2026-03-30.
   - The fee formula changed after March.
   - Maker rebate shares changed.
   - Holding rewards went from 4% to 3.25%.
   - Proposers were whitelisted in Aug 2025.
   - Effects estimated across a rule change need regime-aware design, and per-market parameters must be read
     point-in-time from the API.
9. **Access limits.** SSRN, CEPR and UCD pages returned HTTP 403. For several 2026 papers only the abstract or press
   coverage was read (marked (s)). The full text of each (s) item must be read before it is cited in a protocol.

## 4. Suggested hypotheses for each gap (testable predictions)

These are written to be falsifiable. Thresholds, MDEs and families are left to the registering batch. None changes an
existing protocol. Sports items stay category-agnostic or wait for the user's scope decision.

**G1. Informed long-shot prints in leak-prone classes.**
- **H1a (prospective, live large-print monitor).** Classes are military/defense, geopolitics, and markets decided by a
  small group before a scheduled announcement: awards, official announcements, corporate decisions.
  - Trigger: a single-wallet buy of ≥$2.5k at ≤0.35, with no >5c move in the prior hour.
  - Prediction: the outcome frequency exceeds the trigger price by ≥10 pp, event-clustered.
  - Control: same-class prints of similar size and price from wallets with ≥20 prior markets.
- **H1b.** Entering at the best ask 5 and 30 minutes after the trigger, held to resolution, gives a positive mean net
  P&L. This is the copy-latency test.
- **H1c (negative control).** In sports and 15-minute crypto markets the same trigger has lift ≤ 1.

**G2. YES-optimism / NO-side carry.**
- **H2a (historical, C1/T5 machinery, event-clustered).** In single-name markets (one person, firm or decision; not
  index, statistic or price markets), the YES mid at 1d, 7d and 30d before end exceeds realised YES frequency by ≥3 pp.
  Broad-based (statistic- or price-settled) markets show ≤1 pp.
- **H2b.** The gap is larger for markets whose question is phrased as an event happening ("Will X ...") than for the
  complementary phrasing, holding class fixed.
- **H2c (economic).** Buying NO at the touch at 7d and holding to resolution in the H2a cell has positive mean net P&L
  after the entry fee and half-spread. Report both the maker-fill and the taker variant.
- **Artefact check.** A challenger must first rule out outcome-0 label ordering (the C1/T5 caveat) and survivorship of
  long-listed markets.

**G3. Long-horizon underconfidence × holding rewards.**
- **H3a (CB11-P covers this).** For political markets more than 30 days from end, the calibration slope on resolved
  markets is above 1.2, i.e. favourites are underpriced.
- **H3b.** The slope falls toward 1 as the horizon shrinks (Le's horizon gradient replicated on AREPO's frame).
- **H3c.** In holding-reward-eligible events, the same-horizon slope is closer to 1 than in matched ineligible
  long-dated events, because the reward removes part of the lock-up cost. Low power: register as descriptive, or pool
  across reward eras.

**G4. Option-implied anchor cells** (add to CB10-D/CB13-R before freeze, or as a follow-up).
- **H4a.** For BTC/ETH contracts with option-implied probability below 0.10, the Polymarket mid exceeds it by ≥3 pp on
  average, and the gap halves within 24h without crossing.
- **H4b.** At equal option-implied probability, the gap is larger for Reach/Dip than for Above/Range.
- **H4c.** The gap is larger in the first 48h after listing and on Saturday and Sunday UTC.
- **H4d (economic).** Buying NO at the touch in the H4a cell, held to resolution, has positive mean net P&L after fees.

**G5. Maker P&L by class.**
- **H5a.** Resting bids on the favoured token at 0.70-0.97 in non-sports events, filled and held to resolution, earn a
  positive mean per filled share. Fill realism comes from the CB6-C fill model.
- **H5b.** H5a holds with one unit per parent event, the grouping under which Cardozo's longshot sign flips.
- **H5c.** Maker fills beat taker fills in the same cells by at least the quoted spread, replicating the Kalshi gap on
  Polymarket.
- **H5d.** Maker P&L is higher in single-name than in broad-based classes on the NO side. This is the Bartlett & O'Hara
  pattern, and it re-tests CB13-T's question with a hold-to-resolution mark that needs fewer fills than mark-out
  windows.

**G6. Reward-inclusive maker P&L.**
- **H6a.** For each reward-eligible market and day, compute the expected reward share of a minimum-size two-sided
  quote at half the max spread, using the published score and the observed competing depth from the 2h books.
- **H6b.** Markets in the top quintile of expected reward per unit of expected adverse selection (ECON part B
  mark-outs by class) have positive net maker P&L, and the bottom quintile negative.
- **H6c (mechanism check, CH152).** A within-market change in `rewardsDailyRate` moves quoted depth inside the max
  spread in the same direction within 24h, and the implied reward yield on capital compresses toward the pre-change
  level within a week, as in the Telonex case.

**G7. Skilled-wallet flow.**
- **H7a.** On a training window, classify wallets as skilled with an event-level sign-flip permutation test (the
  Gómez-Cram construction), not by P&L rank.
- **H7b.** On a sealed later window, the net 2h signed flow of skilled wallets predicts the 24h mid change and the
  resolution outcome beyond the price.
- **H7c.** It does better than the WA6 prior-winner feature head-to-head.
- **Falsifier:** out-of-sample persistence of the skilled label is not above the permutation null.

**G8. Clean flow re-test.**
- **H8a.** With on-chain aggressor labels (CB9-S) and fills between wallets in closed counterparty clusters removed
  (Sirolly construction), the E007 / SB2-F4 continuation coefficient rises by ≥50%.
- **Falsifier:** it is unchanged or falls. Then E007's 0.36c gross ceiling stands and flow is closed as a taker
  family.

**G9. Anchor-gap drift** (needs a ≤1-minute collector).
- **H9a.** In Binance/Chainlink-settled Up/Down and Pyth-settled dailies, the gap (model fair value from the live
  reference price minus the Polymarket mid) predicts the next 1-5 minute mid change with slope > 0, steeper in thin
  books.
- **H9b.** The taker version (trade when |gap| > fee + half-spread) has a non-positive mean. This is a pre-declared
  expected null, so a measurement is never mistaken for an edge.

**G10. Sports** (only if the user brings sports into scope).
- **H10a.** In in-play moneylines, bottom-decile-probability contracts in the final 15% of game time resolve below
  price by ≥5 pp (the Whelan and "Yogi Berra" pattern).
- **H10b.** The Polymarket mid at kickoff minus the de-vigged sharp-book close predicts the outcome residual (CLV).
  This needs a lawful odds source.

**Honourable mention: UMA.**
- **HU1 (census first).** Measure dispute and 50-50 rates by class and wording features, and the proposed-against
  side's price path during the 2h liveness.
- **HU2.** Only if there are more than 30 independent disputes: the first proposal is upheld more often than the
  proposed side's liveness price implies (CH03).
- **HU3.** Ex ante, markets with more binding rule conditions show a YES discount. This overlaps CB7-S.
