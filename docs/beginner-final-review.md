# Capable-beginner final review (spec §2)

A numerate but non-expert user deciding what deserves further research.

## Current model view — clear
The market page opens with a plain sentence ("Arepo currently sees moderate evidence of upward
repricing pressure on the Yes outcome") or an honest "not enough evidence" line, then strength,
confidence, the reason and the risks. I can tell what Arepo thinks and how sure it is without
decoding a bare number.

## Opportunity Board — gives a next step
The board defaults to directional views only and tells me how selective it is ("screened N;
M meet the requirements"). Each card leads with the hypothesis and a labelled Research Priority
(High/Medium/Low with a one-line meaning), so I know what to look at first. Tags explain themselves
in a popover (definition, why it matters, family); the old "Full definition" link that went nowhere
is gone.

## Signal Lab — no longer misleading
Each signal now says whether it "Qualifies for a directional model view" or is "Observational
only", with the reason. Previously it looked like every anomaly was an opportunity; now the
relationship to the board and market page is explicit, and Yes/No are consolidated to one per
market.

## Replay — builds appropriate (not false) confidence
Replay answers "if I'd followed Arepo's top five at this cut-off, what happened?" It shows the
funnel (why only a few reconstruct), the top five with the evidence known at the time, and a
comparison against simple baselines. Importantly it tells me plainly when the sample is too small
to prove anything, so I am not misled by a 3-of-5 result. That honesty makes me trust it more.

## How It Works — easy to navigate
There is now a left menu that sticks and highlights the section I am reading, the text is darker,
and the cards are smaller so it does not feel like scrolling through big empty boxes. It explains
directional views, abstention, Research Priority, strength, confidence, evidence families, Replay
modes and accounts.

## Sign-in and alert value — clear brand and offer
Sign-in leads with the Arepo symbol and wordmark and the brand story, then a short line on what a
free account gives me (alerts, saved markets, preferences). The form is secondary. It is obvious
what Arepo is and why I might sign up.

## Small remaining wish
A one-line "what to check next" prompt on each opportunity (news, resolution rules) would round it
off, but the market page already points me to verify things myself. Overall the product now tells
me what to look at, how confident it is, and when it does not know — which is what I wanted.
